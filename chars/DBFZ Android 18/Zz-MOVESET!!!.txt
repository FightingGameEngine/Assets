Lista de Movimientos.

- Taunt                 = Start

** Normals:

- Combo 1               = a
- Combo 2               = b

- Grab                  = a or b          (close to the opponent)

- Power Charge          = D,Start         (Hold)

** Specials:
  
- Stand Special         = c               (Hold)
- Side Special          = F,c
- Down Special          = D,c             (Hold)

- Air Atacks            = a,b or c
- Air Special           = D,c

** Supers:
 
- Kienzan               = D,DF,F,a        Power = 1000
- infinite bullets      = D,DF,F,b        Power = 2000 (Hold)
- Heruzu Furasshu       = D,DF,F,c        Power = 3000 (Unguardable)

** TEAM MODE:
  
- Change                = a+b             Power = 500
- Call Helper           = b+c             Power = 500
- Hyper Dual            = D,DF,F,a+b      Power = 3000  

** Counter Ability:

- Teleport              = B,c             Power = 1500 (when you get hurt)
