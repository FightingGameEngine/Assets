Spider-Man originally by Seth Zankuten, edited by Koldskool

Movelist:
Specials:
Web Ball: QCF, P (also usuable in air)
Spider Sting: DP, P (press P after move connects for a follow-up attack)
Web Swing: QCB, K (also usuable in air)
Web Throw: HCB, P (punch button pressed determines direction)

Supers (consume 1 level):
Crawler Assault: QCFx2, K
Maximum Spider: QCFx2, P (also usuable in air)
Ultimate Web Throw: QCBx2, P

MAX Super (consumes 3 levels):
Separation Anxiety: QCFx2, KK

Others:
Dodge: LP+LK
Roll: Forward+LP+LK
Power Charge: Hold MP+MK
Counterattack: (While guarding) HP+HK (consumes 1 level)
Super Jump: Quickly press Down, then Up. Pressing Up while dashing will also perform a Super Jump.
Aerial Rave: Press Up after standing MP or standing HK connects. (Allows combos to continue in the air.)
