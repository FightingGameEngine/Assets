==[Option]=================================================================================================================

  You can set some of options in the UMVC_Hulk___Option.txt file. Plaease check it out once at least with text editor.

  The Settings of Short-cut
  The Level of X-Factor
  The Setting of Own Life-handy-capped Damage
  The Setting of Enemy's Life-handy-capped Damage
  The Maximum number of X-Factor
  The time limit of X-Factor
  The max amount of Vital Source at X-Factor
  

==[System]=================================================================================================================

  This character is made like Ultimate MARVEL VS CAPCOM 3.
  However, 3 gauges Hyper Combo, Asist Attack, and X-Factor have not been made yet.
  

==[AI]=====================================================================================================================

    Dedicated Counter-Plan Switch
    Extremely Faster Reaction Switch
    Frequencies of the Artzs
