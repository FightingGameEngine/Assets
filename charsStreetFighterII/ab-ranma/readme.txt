--==            Movelist            ==--

Ranma-chan is a 4 button character (HP and HK are not used).


  --==**  Basics

u (air)          - Air Jump
LP+MP            - Fall recovery
f f              - Forward Dash
b b              - Back Dash
S                - Taunt

Taunts can be aborted after some time, however, you'll then miss out on a nice power boost.

LK (close)              - Light High Kick
MK (close)              - Handstand Kick (hits twice)
f + MP                  - Forward Knuckle
f + MP (close)          - Ellbow Attack
P (during forward dash) - Dash Punch
f/b + MK (close)        - Throw


  --==**  Specials

d + MK (air only)       - Dive Kick
	Knocks down airborne opponents.

d, df, f, P             - Moko Takabisha
	A short ranged projectile. Strong version travels slightly further.

d, db, b, P             - Hiryu Shoten Ha
	A tornado which knocks opponents up into the sky.

P P P (tap)             - Kachu Tenshin Amaguriken
	A series of rapid punches.

d, df, f, K             - Rolling Ellbow Attack
	Ranma rolls forward, then hits her enemy hard with her ellbow.


  --==**  Supers (require 1 bar of power)

d, df, f,  LP + MP      - Gou Moko Takabisha
	Stronger version of Ranma's projectile attack.

d, db, b,  LP + MP      - Hiryu Korin Dan
	Starts just like a Hiryu Shoten Ha, but if it hits, Ranma will jump up and come crashing down on her opponent.
