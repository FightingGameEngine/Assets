Sei says,

The original idea, when I started to work on Jason,
was to create an ambiguous character,
an anti-hero belonging to the bat-family.
Something similar to Batman, but without cape and with guns.
The base was originally a mix of Captain America and Maxima,
not a great result, then with the help of ANC,
we focused on Charlie and Maxima, to give Jason a more acrobatic look,
and then, after three or four changes of style and with the help of our team,
we can introduce the first version of Jason Todd.
This version is based on the comics as "Batman: Under The Hood",
"52" and "The Lost Days", basically everything before the "Battle for the Cowl".

A new version, the masked one, is currently in production. So stay tuned!

[:Half measure:]
Stream of dual gunfire,
angled low,high, or anti-air.
Punch strength determines angle

-Crouch   - D,DF,F + Lp
 
-Anti-air - D,DF,F + Mp

-Stand    - D,DF,F + Hp

[:Reflex:]
--D,DB,B + P: (Hold P to stay at wall) 
Wall Cling, bomb toss..

[:Stolen Goods:]
Jason tosses variable stuff from his jacket,
based on punch strength

-Knife     - D,DF,F + Lk

-Stun dart - D,DF,F + Mk

-grenade   - D,DF,F + Hk

[:Talent:] (GROUND/AIR)
--D,DB,B + K:
Somersault backflip kick.
