--| Shimo by Antagonist |------------------------------------------

Yatagarasu character for M.U.G.E.N.
Custom version
This character is Open Source. Feel free to use any part of it or modify it. 

--| Commands |-----------------------------------------------------

   U	  A - Light Kick	B - Strong Kick
 B   F
   D	  X - Light Slash	Y - Strong Slash


--| System Related Commands |--------------------------------------

 Forward Dash		Tap FF
 Back Dash		Tap BB
 Throw			a + x
 Leap Attack		x + y
 Guard Break Attack	b + y
 Standing Parry		Tap F when attacked
 Crouching Parry	Tap D when attacked low
 Air Parry		Tap F when attacked in air


--| Special Moves |------------------------------------------------

 Shingetsu		D,DF,F + x or y
   > Middle Slash	x
   > Lower Slash	a
   > Upper Slash	y
   > Stop		b
 Shinko Enzan		(In Air) D,DB,B + x or y


--| EX Moves (Requires half powerbar) |----------------------------

 Shingetsu EX		D,DF,F + x+y
   > Same followups as normal version
 Shinko Enzan EX	(In Air) D,DB,B + x+y


--| Super Moves (Requires one powerbar) |--------------------------

 Kantan Enzan		D,DF,F,D,DF,F + x or y
 Issen Kousa		D,DF,F,D,DF,F + a or b
