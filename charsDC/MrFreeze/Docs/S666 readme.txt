S666 ~~  Author Blog

	I started this work just a little before I finished The Phantom, but it was a little mistake. I had see Mr. Freeze on the frontpage list of MMV forum and though that was that Mr. Freeze by Skhsato, there I already wanted to rework him because I like the sprites, but actually was the Elecbyte's Mr. Freeze. Before I notice, I already have extract all Sprites and started to code that one haha
	Since the first time I download that Mr. Freeze I feel that he needed be recoded so I already wanted to do it XD
	As many people have said, he are not faithful with the real Mr. Freeze (with a heavy armor and non-muscle body) but I still tried my best to focus on him using only his Ice Weapon, all the moves was made with that in mind.
	My goal to his gameplay is basically "Keep distance from the enemy until frost him then approach + combo, and back for distance." Hope I have achieve that goal ;)
	
	A few days after I post the preview video (Pyro vs Mr. Freeze) came the news about the others Mr. Freeze wips. They are much more faithful than that, so with that mind, I decided to go a little more distance from the already knowed Mr. Freeze and take as inspiration Mr. & Mrs Freeze (DC Rebirth), then I did a little version of Mrs. Freeze that match with the rest of character ;)

----------------------------------------------------------------------
For the ones who came later. The character *code* is fully open to spriteswap/updates. I cant say the same for the art and sound since it is not my work, I only did the char. ;)
