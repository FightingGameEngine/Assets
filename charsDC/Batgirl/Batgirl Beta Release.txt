When i first started do build this char, i made it just by curiosity, When i doscovered MUGEN at 3 years ago, i didn't have 
any idea that someday i wold be working on a mugen wip, so here i am.
I saw Mole's batgirl version based on Cammy's sprites and at first my goal was to enhance that wip, but in time i've decided
not to use any of that sprites, even because they're not mine and the owner could be working on it yet. Them i've decided
tho begin from zero, using just the idea of Cammy as a base, but also planning to use as many diferente bases as i could to
maker her as more different from Cammy as it could be, so i begin to look for another female char in fighting games that
can have moves that could fit on my char, and the list begins to grown: Yuri sakazaki(CvS), Whip (Kof), Arina (Waku Waku 7),
Lilith (Darkstalkers), Jill valentine (CvS), Karin (CvS), Tia (Breakers revenge),Ingrid (Capcom Fighting Evolution)
and i intend to use some Ibuki (SF3) sprites that i'm still working on.

This Beta version is about 70% of what i have in mind for this Wip. I send her to Zvitor to him to make his modifications and
and this version you have in hands now have a lot of Zvitor's work, since the palettes, that are all his works to some
get hits that he fixed (mine was a real mess), not to mention the Hypers, that are all his ideas.
