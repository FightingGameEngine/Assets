Who is Professor Zoom?
---------------------------------------------
Eobard Thawne was a brilliant scientist born in the 25th century, where Barry Allen's heroism as the Flash is the stuff of legend. Seeking to emulate his idol, 
Thawne traveled back in time to meet the Flash, but learned instead that his destiny was to become the Reverse-Flash, Allen's greatest nemesis. 
Rendered unstable by this knowledge, Thawne set out to erase Barry Allen from history and establish himself as the one true Flash.

Why Professor Zoom?
--------------------------------------------
To be perfectly honest, I'm not a real big fan of Professor Zoom, though I love the reverse flash concept I think personally for me he's the weakest one.
I made the character however because I thought I could make a character based on Zoom (hunter zolomen) with the sprites, and after reading about the new 52
Professor Zoom, (who has the same time manipulating powers as Zolomen) I figured it was a great time to make the character.

Basic movement and attacks (Normal Mode)
---------------------------------------------
XYZ
ABC
Basic attacks, punches on top, kicks on bottom.

F,F or 2 Punches
Dash forward

B,B or B + 2 Punches
Dash Backward.

D,U or 2 Kicks
Super Jump

F+Z/B+Z (must be close to Player 2)
Punch Grab
Zoom Punches you in the gut a few times, then dashes into you, he knocks you in a different direction depending on if you pushed forward of back.

D+Z
Launcher
Zoom does a lunging swipe attack that launches the opponent into the air and can be super jumped out of for an aerial rave.

Special Moves (Normal Mode)
---------------------------------------------

D,DF,F + Punch 
Lightning Stab
Zoom Dashes forward really fast and does a few stabs with his lighting staff. 
The stronger the attack button, the further he dashes and the more stabs he does.

F, DF, D, DB, B + punch 
Rapid Punch
Zoom unleashes a rapid series of punches, if connects the opponent get hit a bit then knocked into a wall bounce.. 
X hits 6 times, Y hits 10 Times, and Z hits 14 times.

F,D,DF + Punch (air OK)
Speed force Slam
Zoom jumps Forward and does a slaming that cause a huge lightning bolt. 
This Move is both an OTG, and an anti-air move. 
The Distance Zoom jumps is based on the Button Pressed, With Z being the Furthest and X Being the Shortest. 

D, D + Kick
Speed Dodge
Zoom Vibrates his body super fast to dodge anything. The button pressed changes the duration of the dodge with z being the longest and x being the shortest.

Hyper Moves (Normal Mode)
---------------------------------------------

D, DF, F + 2 Punches
Terminal Velocity(Level 1)
Zoom dashes forward really fast. If it hits he slashes the opponnet with his spear, if not (or blocked) it does only one hit that leaves him vulnerable.

D, DB, B + 2 Punches
Sonic Boom Snap
Zoom Snaps his fingers creating a shockwave on certain parts of the screen.

D, DF, F + 2 Kicks
Futretech Bomb (Level 1)
Zoom dashes forward really fast. If it hits he plants a bomb on the opponent and detonates it.

F, D, DF + 2 Punches
Reverse Hurricane (Level 1)
Zoom Spins around really fast cause a huge tornado to surround him. it then travels straight up.

F,DF, D, DB, B + 2 Punches
Swift Hand of Death(Level 3)
Zoom Grabs right in front of him and impales the opponent with his hand.

D, DB, B + 2 Kicks
Negative Speedforce fury. (Level 3)
Zoom dashes forward really fast. If it hits he does his rapid punch, stab and then a really huge lightning slam to finish the opponent off.

Your power meter will drain during this hyper for the amount of time stopped and you can use other hypers during it, if you do though the time stopped is reduced.
